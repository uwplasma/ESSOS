\section{Diagnostic pressure-response results}

We evaluated a fixed-coil vacuum reference and positive-pressure free-boundary
VMEX continuation at three radii. The reference uses the signed toroidal flux
measured by direct tracing of the vacuum coil field. Each continuation retains
that flux label, the pressure-profile shape, and zero enclosed toroidal current.
The vacuum transform and interface checks pass their predeclared acceptance
gates, and the pressure equilibria converge with an active exterior solve.

The finite-pressure axes do not presently agree with the untuned first-order
near-axis response. The symmetry-plane radial displacement has the opposite
sign, and the measured axis-length change does not reproduce the predicted
positive slope. The measured response also changes materially with radial
resolution. The current data therefore establish a numerical discrepancy, not
a validated pressure-shift result. Figures~\ref{fig:shafranov-symmetry-plane}
through~\ref{fig:shafranov-radial-resolution} document the comparison without
fitted amplitude corrections. We withhold numerical manuscript claims until
pressure-step convergence, radial and Fourier resolution, and interface error
are separated.
